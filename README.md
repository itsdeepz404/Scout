# 🧭 Waymark — Open Learning, Research & Opportunity Hub

> **Learn it. Build it. Grow.**  
> A clean, modern platform designed to help students and self-learners discover top-tier educational materials, structured open classes, and real-world opportunities **without spending money**.

---

## 💡 Core Philosophy: Learning & Research First

1. **Free Open Knowledge for Everyone**:
   - Educational qualifications are **never** required to access learning resources.
   - Curated high-impact YouTube courses, official documentation, interactive labs, and research paper databases.
   - Every resource is clearly labeled as **`Beginner` 🌱**, **`Intermediate` 📘**, or **`Advanced` ⚡** so learners can bridge skill gaps without confusion.
   - **"What you will learn from this source"**: Every card explicitly details the skills, concepts, and practical abilities acquired.

2. **Personalized Recommendations**:
   - Takes your **experience level** and **preferred learning style** (videos, hands-on projects, reading/docs, or a mix) to surface a dedicated **"⭐ Recommended for You"** shelf.
   - All other resources remain easily accessible and searchable.

3. **100% Free & Minimal Cost Focus**:
   - Designed to eliminate financial barriers to learning and growth.
   - One-click **"100% Free Only"** toggle instantly filters out any paid materials.

4. **Locality-Smart Without Artificial Limits**:
   - Enter your city/region to surface nearby study circles and regional science fairs.
   - **Global & online resources are never restricted** by locality.

5. **Two Clear Next Paths**:
   At the end of your study plan, choose how you want to expand:
   - 🎓 **"See Classes Available"**: Free structured university courses (MIT OpenCourseWare, Harvard CS50, Stanford Online) with syllabus breakdowns and audit tips.
   - 🏆 **"Want to See Your Opportunities?"**: Global, national, and local competitions (USACO, ISEF, Wharton), summer research fellowships (MIT BWSI, Stanford AIMI, Simons), internships, and micro-grants. Educational qualifications are requested here only to highlight matching opportunities.

## 🎨 Visual Design
- **Modern Blue Palette**: Deep midnight navy, royal blue, and sky blue accents designed to feel calm, inspiring, and clutter-free.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Seamless toggle with persistent preference stored locally.
- **My Saved Tracker**: Bookmark resources and track application milestones (`Saved`, `In Progress`, `Applied`, `Accepted`).
- **Markdown Export & Print**: Export your complete study guide and opportunity plan to Markdown or print to PDF.
