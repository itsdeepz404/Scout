# 🧭 Scout — Open Learning, Research & Opportunities Hub

> **Learn it. Build it. Grow.**  
> A clean, interactive platform designed to help students and self-learners discover top-tier educational materials, structured open classes (online and offline), and real-world opportunities **without spending money**.

---

## 💡 Core Philosophy: Learning & Research First

### 1. Free Open Knowledge for Everyone (No Educational Barriers)
- **No educational qualifications are required to access learning resources.** Anyone of any age, background, or grade can learn freely.
- Curated high-impact video courses, official technical documentation, interactive coding sandboxes, and open research paper databases.
- Every resource is clearly labeled as **`Beginner` 🌱**, **`Intermediate` 📘**, or **`Advanced` ⚡** so learners can bridge skill gaps without confusion.
- **"What you will learn from this source"**: Every card explicitly outlines the exact concepts, tools, and practical skills you will gain.

### 2. Personalized Recommendations
- Takes your **current experience level** and **preferred learning style** (visual videos, hands-on projects, reading/docs, or a balanced mix) to feature a dedicated **"⭐ Recommended for You"** shelf at the top.
- All other resources remain fully accessible, searchable, and filterable.

### 3. 100% Free & Minimal Cost Focus
- Built specifically to eliminate financial barriers to learning and achievement.
- A single-click **"100% Free Only"** toggle instantly filters out any resources, classes, or opportunities that carry a cost.

### 4. Locality-Smart Without Artificial Limits
- Enter your city or region to surface local in-person classes, public library workshops, and regional science fairs near you.
- **Global and online resources are never restricted** by locality.

## 🌿 Two Clear Next Paths (At the Conclusion of Your Study Plan)

At the bottom of your learning plan, Scout presents two optional, distinct paths to take your knowledge further:

### 🎓 1. "See Classes Available" (Online & In-Person / Offline)
Classes are not just from elite universities — they come from diverse, accessible educators:
- **Independent Creators & YouTubers**: Free masterclasses and project cohorts (e.g. Andrej Karpathy's Deep Learning series, Traversy Media's full-stack bootcamps, Ninja Nerd's illustrated physiology).
- **Teachers & Community Mentors**: Interactive live cohorts with worksheets and volunteer mentors (e.g. freeCodeCamp live cohorts, Khan Academy mastery tracks).
- **Local In-Person / Offline Workshops**: Free hands-on classes held near you at public libraries, community maker spaces, and university extension centers.
- **University Open Courses**: Free audit options from Harvard CS50, MIT OpenCourseWare, and Stanford Online.
- **Explicit Delivery Indicators**: Every class card highlights whether it is **`🌐 Online (Virtual / Livestream)`** or **`📍 Offline (In-Person)`**, with a one-click filter to switch between formats.

### 🏆 2. "Want to See Your Opportunities?"
- **Educational qualifications are asked only here**: Select your stage (`High School`, `Undergraduate / College`, or `Lifelong Learner / Open to All`) to highlight opportunities you directly qualify for.
- All opportunities remain visible for browsing, with an optional filter for your qualifications.
- **Detailed Opportunity Breakdown on Every Card**:
  - **🎓 Who Qualifies / Eligibility**: Grade levels, age limits, and prerequisites.
  - **💻 How Conducted**: 100% Virtual, residential campus lab, or hybrid with local mentorship.
  - **📍 Where & When**: Program locations, seasonal dates, and annual application deadlines.
  - **What Happens**: A clear description of what participants build, research, or compete in.
  - **Cost & Aid**: 100% Free entry, paid monthly stipends ($1,500 – $3,300+), and cash grants ($1,000 – $100,000).
- **Multi-Status Application Tracker**: Track your opportunities across `📌 Saved`, `⏳ In Progress`, `🚀 Applied`, and `🎉 Accepted`.

## 🎨 Visual Design & Features

- **Modern Blue Palette**: Deep midnight navy, royal blue, and sky blue accents on a clean slate/ice background (`#F8FAFC`). Designed to feel calm, focused, and uncluttered.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Built-in toggle with persistent theme memory saved locally.
- **Interactive Study Roadmap**: 4-phase milestone checklist with persistent progress tracking.
- **Export Study Guide**: Export your complete study plan to Markdown with one click, or use the print-optimized view to save a clean PDF.
- **100% Private**: All bookmarks and progress are stored entirely in your browser's `localStorage`. No logins or external tracking required.

## 💻 Local Usage

Scout requires zero build tools, node_modules, or servers:
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Double-click `index.html` or `chatbot.html` in your browser.

---

## 🚀 Hosting on GitHub Pages (Public Access)

1. Create a public repository on GitHub (e.g. `scout`).
2. Upload `index.html`, `chatbot.html`, and `README.md`.
3. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Pages** &rarr; select `main` branch / `root` &rarr; click **Save**.
4. Your website will be live at:  
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`
